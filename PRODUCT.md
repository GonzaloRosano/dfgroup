# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (user-requested), with React islands where needed for Three.js / WebGL. Package manager: **pnpm**. Build intentionally unminified (no aggressive minify/compress). Deploy target undecided.

## Users

Prospective collaborators, clients, and creative peers evaluating whether Dark Feather Group is the right studio to work with — scanning identity, craft, and how to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

A public web presence for Dark Feather Group (also Dark Feather / DF Group): a creative collective / digital studio covering design, development, art, and production. Success means a visitor understands who they are, trusts the craft signal, and knows how to get in touch or start a collaboration.

## Positioning

A noir-modern digital atelier: atmosphere and technical craft (including WebGL) as proof of capability, not a generic agency brochure. Exact differentiator beyond name + noir commitment remains open until the team supplies real case work.

## Operating Context

Multi-page marketing site (not a single-page scroll). Visitor path: land → choose a DF line page → contact. Routes: `/`, `/hosting`, `/series`, `/atelier`, `/voices`, `/grupo`, `/contacto`. No authenticated product workflows.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Marketing / identity site only (no app backend required for v1).
- Must support Astro + Three.js (client-side WebGL via React island if needed).
- Real project case studies, team roster, and contact channels not yet supplied — treat as open; do not invent commercial claims, clients, or metrics.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Dark Feather Group**; acceptable short forms: **Dark Feather**, **DF Group**.
- Visual constraint (user-binding): **Noir style**, modern.
- Language of the site: Spanish-first (brief and conversation in Spanish); English dual-language undecided.
- Product-line naming pattern: full **Dark Feather {Offer}**, short **DF {Offer}**.
- Confirmed product lines (v1 messaging): **DF Hosting**, **DF Series**, **DF Atelier**, **DF Voices**.

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo: `public/logo.svg` (feather mark). Used in header, footer, and favicon.
- No photography, case studies, testimonials, or real contact endpoints yet. Synthetic portfolio / demonstration material must stay labeled.

## Product Principles

1. Atmosphere is part of the proof — the site should feel like the work, not describe it from outside.
2. Prefer demonstration (motion, WebGL, crafted composition) over claim lists.
3. Never invent clients, awards, or metrics; open facts stay open or labeled synthetic.
4. Keep the path to contact short and obvious.
5. Honor the noir brief without collapsing into generic “neon on black” agency defaults unless the committed visual world requires it.
